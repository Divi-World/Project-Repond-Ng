FAQ

Before you send your form.

What do I need to send?

Your questionnaire link, respondent type, number of responses, approximate length and deadline.

What if I need a specific respondent group?

Tell us the eligibility criteria first. Specialised audiences may have different pricing.

Where do responses appear?

Respondents complete the online questionnaire link you provide, so submissions appear in your existing form system.

Can you guarantee my grade?

No. ProjectRespond NG coordinates respondents only. Academic grading and defense outcomes remain outside the service.
